Honesty note: GTA 6 is days old and no guide here has been checked in the game. Every guide below is our own reading of the official trophy description, and it says so beside each trophy. Times and effort marks are our estimates, not promises. What the official list states (name, description, grade, PSN count) is shown as PlayStation publishes it.

The plan: six shifts. Each shift is one play session with a job list. Clock a trophy in when you earn it; the timecard above keeps the count. Do the now verdicts in their shift, leave the later ones for shift 5, and grab the anytime ones whenever they are near.

    The Grind board tracks every trophy the game counts: stunts, races, cards, WAiNK tags, money and more. Tap + or − as you play; the counters save in your browser beside your checklist. Targets are the counts PSN tracks, from the official list.

    The alarm list: 15 trophies whose moment is a one-time scene or a strict condition in a single attempt. Missable here means what the official description implies: if the moment passes or the condition fails, replay the mission or reload a save from before it. They are ordered by shift, so you can see each alarm before its shift starts.

      Save & settings

      Progress saves in this browser (localStorage) as you tick trophies and tap the Grind board. Export a backup file before you clear browser data, and import it back here.
